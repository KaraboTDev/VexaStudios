About

VexaStudios is a small, independent website studio helping businesses present themselves clearly online.

VexaStudios exists to help businesses establish a professional online presence without the friction, overhead and vague promises that often come with larger agencies. The focus is simple: clear messaging, good design, strong structure and a website that supports enquiries and growth.

We work with businesses that value clarity, quality and honest communication. Every project is designed around the business behind it, with a focus on practical outcomes rather than flashy but unnecessary features.

Our long-term vision is to grow into a broader digital studio, but our immediate focus remains website development and supporting small businesses with a modern, professional online presence.

FAQ

Common questions before you start.

Do you provide a full website package?

Yes. We can handle design, copy guidance, page structure, responsiveness and the technical setup needed to launch a professional site.

What is included in the project price?

It includes design, page build, responsive layout, contact functionality, basic SEO foundations and a defined number of revisions based on the package selected.

Do you include domain and hosting?

Domain and hosting are typically separate from the design and build fee. We can guide you on the best setup for your budget and business needs.

Are the project prices final?

These are starting prices for typical website scopes. Final pricing depends on your needs, content requirements, custom features and delivery timeline.
