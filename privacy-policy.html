Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 November 2026

This policy explains how VexaStudios handles personal information when you visit this website or contact us about a project. VexaStudios is based in South Africa.

Information you provide

If you use the project enquiry form, you may provide your name, business name, email address, optional WhatsApp number, website type, approximate budget and project description. You may also contact us directly by email or WhatsApp.

How enquiries are sent

The enquiry form prepares an email in your device’s default email application. The website itself does not submit form details to a VexaStudios server or store them in a website database. Your email or messaging provider will process the information when you send it, under that provider’s own privacy terms.

Please do not include sensitive personal information in a project enquiry.

How we use information

Information you choose to send us is used to respond to your enquiry, discuss and scope a possible project, provide a quote, and communicate about work you ask us to do. We do not sell your personal information.

Website providers and external content

This website is hosted by a third-party hosting provider. Like many hosting services, it may process technical information such as requests, browser details or IP addresses to deliver and protect the website. Some pages may load fonts or images from external providers, which may receive technical connection data from your browser. Those providers handle data under their own privacy policies.

Retention and security

Messages you send may be kept in our email or messaging accounts for as long as reasonably needed to respond, manage a project, keep business records or resolve a dispute. We take reasonable steps to protect information under our control, but no online transmission or storage method can be guaranteed completely secure.

Your choices and requests

You can choose not to provide information, although we may then be unable to respond to or assess your project enquiry. You may contact us to request access to, correction or deletion of personal information we hold, subject to applicable legal requirements.

This policy is intended as general website information and is not legal advice. It may be updated as our website or practices change.
